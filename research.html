I am a Royal Society University Research Fellow and Professor of Probability, and Director of Prob-L@B at the University of Bath. From April 2013 until April 2016 I was a Bath Prize Fellow and EPSRC Postdoctoral Fellow.

Here is a brief employment history: from October 2012 to March 2013 I was a research fellow in the Department of Statistics at the University of Warwick. Previously I spent a year as a postdoc at McGill University, in Montreal, working with Louigi Addario-Berry. For six months until June 2011 I was a postdoc at the Weierstrass Institute in Berlin, part of the "Interacting Random Systems" group working under Wolfgang König. Before that I was a postdoc at Université Pierre et Marie Curie (UPMC) - Paris 6 - and a member of the Équipe Processus Stochastiques, part of LPMA, where I worked with Julien Berestycki, and was supported by ANR MADCOF (grant ANR-08-BLAN-0220-01). Until the end of 2009 I was a PhD student at the University of Bath, under the supervision of Simon Harris.
