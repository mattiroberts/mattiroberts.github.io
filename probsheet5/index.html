1 Bookwork questions

1.1 Question 1

Which of the following are always true? For those that are not always true, give a condition under which they are true.

  1. For events \(E\) and \(F\), \[\mathbb{P}(E\cup F) = \mathbb{P}(E)+\mathbb{P}(F).\]

  2. For events \(E\) and \(F\), \[\mathbb{P}(E\cap F) = \mathbb{P}(E)\mathbb{P}(F).\]

  3. For events \(E\) and \(F\), \[\mathbb{P}(F) = \mathbb{P}(F\cap E) + \mathbb{P}(F\cap E^c).\]

  4. For events \(E_1\), \(E_2\) and \(F\) such that \(\mathbb{P}(E_1)>0\) and \(\mathbb{P}(E_2)>0\), \[\mathbb{P}(F) = \mathbb{P}(E_1)\mathbb{P}(F|E_1) + \mathbb{P}(E_2)\mathbb{P}(F|E_2).\]

  5. For events \(E\) and \(F\) such that \(\mathbb{P}(E)\in(0,1)\), \[\mathbb{P}(F) = \mathbb{P}(E)\mathbb{P}(F|E) + \mathbb{P}(E^c)\mathbb{P}(F|E^c).\]

  6. For events \(E\) and \(F\), \[\mathbb{P}(E | F) = \frac{\mathbb{P}(E\cap F)}{\mathbb{P}(F)}.\]

1.2 Question 2

The probability that Dave’s partner is angry at him is 0.5. If Dave has done something wrong, then the probability that his partner is angry at him increases to 0.8. Dave estimates that at any given time, the probability that he has done something wrong is 0.4.

Dave observes that his partner is angry at him. What is the probability that he has done something wrong?
